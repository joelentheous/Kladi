Your fit card

One full-length photo and your height. Cladi turns it into six measurements, keeps them on this phone, and uses them on every listing.

  • Stand straight, feet together, facing the camera.
  • Hold your arms a little away from your sides so your waist shows.
  • Wear fitted clothes. A baggy hoodie hides your real shape.
  • Get your whole body in, head to heels. Ask a friend to hold the phone at waist height.

Your photo never leaves this phone. Cladi keeps only the numbers, and you can delete them anytime.

Looking for you in the photo…

Drag each dot to the edge of your body

The black bars go on the top of your head and the floor under your heels. Pairs of dots sit on the outside edge of your shoulders, chest, waist and hips. The last dot goes where your legs meet.

Measured from a photo, these are usually within 2 to 4 cm. If you have a tape measure, check your waist and fix it here.
