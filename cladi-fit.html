    Why “between sizes” happens.

    The chart isn’t lying, it’s just answering a different question. It tells you what the garment measures, not what your body needs.

    +1 in

    Fabric needs room

    A non-stretch waistband should measure a little more than your body, about one finger to two. A garment that matches your waist exactly is a size that fights you all day.

    ±0.8

    Charts have slack

    Sellers measure by hand, so each piece can be off by half an inch or more. Cladi Fit shows that wobble on the tape so you can see when a size is too close to call.

    53%

    Fit is why clothes go back

    Apparel retailers name size and fit as the top reason for returns. For a buyer in Nairobi, a wrong size from abroad doesn’t get returned. It gets given away.

    Thrift has no size chart. So we make one per piece.

    A Gikomba jacket might carry a UK label, an old shrink, and a tailor’s fix. On Cladi, sellers measure the actual item once, and every buyer sees how it fits them.

    1. Lay it flat, measure three linesWaist, hips and inseam for trousers. Chest, length and sleeve for tops. A tape and thirty seconds.
    2. Cladi doubles and checks themFlat widths become full measurements, and numbers that look off get flagged before the listing goes live.
    3. Buyers see their own fit lineAnyone with a saved fit card sees “fits you” or “tight at the hips” right on the listing.
